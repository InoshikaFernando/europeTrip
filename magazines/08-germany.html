No. 08 · A Letter from the Road
Welcome to
Bavaria.

We saw Germany in two doses. Once on the way south to Lourdes — a night in the village of Ottobeuren, with its astonishing rococo basilica. Once on the way back home — a night at Aufkirchen, with the BMW headquarters and the Olympic Park within an hour's drive of Munich Airport.

Germany is not just Berlin. Bavaria is its own thing — Catholic, baroque, beer-drinking, slightly suspicious of the rest of the country, deeply proud of its yellow-and-white striped flag.

This issue is short, because we were short on time. But the Ottobeuren basilica alone could fill a magazine.

— The Munasinghes, 2026

COVER STORY · OTTOBEUREN · DAY 19
A village church
that became
a masterpiece.
The Basilica of Saints Alexander and Theodore at Ottobeuren is, by some accounts, the greatest rococo interior ever built. Pope John XXIII raised it to minor basilica status in 1959.
Mon 27 Jul 2026Ottobeuren, Allgäu90 min · interior + crypt

HERO PHOTO · TBD
The Ottobeuren Basilica nave. Frescos by Johann Jakob Zeiller. Plasterwork by Johann Michael Feichtmayr. Completed 1766.

Ottobeuren is a village of 8,000 people in southwestern Bavaria, an hour south of Munich. It has a tiny main street, a brewery, and a basilica that punches a thousand times above its weight. The Benedictine abbey was founded here in 764 AD. Its current church was begun in 1737, consecrated in 1766, and decorated over four decades by some of the finest baroque artists in Europe. The ceiling frescos cover 1,800 square metres. The pipe organ has 5,000 pipes. The acoustics are perfect.

München — 'by the monks' — a market by the river built by black-robed brothers in 1158.

We stayed at the AKZENT Brauerei Hotel Hirsch, a few streets from the basilica. We arrived in the late afternoon, walked over for vespers, and were the only family in the building. The kids spent twenty minutes craning their necks at the ceiling without making a sound. Aviann whispered "is this a church or a palace?" We did not have an answer that was both true and short.

A BAVARIAN NIGHT · DAY 28
In a 432-year-old
beer hall.
The Hofbräuhaus opened in 1589 as the royal Bavarian brewery. Today it seats 3,000 in oompah-band noise. The pretzels are the size of dinner plates. The beer comes in 1-litre steins.
Wed 5 Aug 2026Hofbräuhaus, Platzl 92 hrs · 4 mains + 4 pretzels
📷 images/germany/day-28-hofbrauhaus.jpg
HERO PHOTO · TBD
The Schwemme — the largest hall of the Hofbräuhaus. 3,000 seats. Vaulted ceiling painted in 1897. Same recipe since 1589.

You don't go to the Hofbräuhaus for nuance. You go for the spectacle. Long oak tables packed with strangers. Hot pretzels delivered by waitresses in dirndl. A brass band playing oompah at full volume. A roast pork knuckle the size of a baby's head. We brought all three kids. They had never seen this much beer being drunk in one place. They had never seen a band like this. They sat on the bench and watched.

Munich has rebuilt itself after every catastrophe — and never lost the beer.

We ordered Schweinshaxe (roast pork knuckle), Weisswurst (white sausage), Brezn (giant pretzels), and Apfelstrudel. Avin ate his half-pretzel, then most of his sister's. We watched the band. We left tipsy and happy. Aviann said, on the walk back to the U-Bahn, "I think they have fun here all the time."

REFLECTIONS · GERMANY
Onion domes,
oompah bands,
and Bavarian basilicas.

Germany is more than Munich, and Bavaria is more than the Hofbräuhaus. But our two passes through this country — Ottobeuren on the way south, Aufkirchen + Munich on the way home — gave us a flavour. Catholic, baroque, beer-loving, and proud.

The Ottobeuren basilica will stay with the kids longer than the beer hall did. We have a photograph of all three children staring at the ceiling, faces pointed up, mouths slightly open. We will print it large.

Favourite moment
Ottobeuren ceiling at 17:00. Empty church. Sun coming through the high windows.
Hardest moment
Hofbräuhaus noise. Avin covered his ears within ten minutes.
If we come back…
Berlin, Dresden, the Romantic Road. Neuschwanstein. The Black Forest in autumn.
— Navin, Lana, Avisha, Aviann & Avin Munasinghe
