📍 Location
Where this stop sits in the journey
Milan half-day in the morning (Duomo + rooftop terrace, Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, Castello Sforzesco courtyards, lunch in Brera), leave Milan ~13:30. Stop at Lake Maggiore (Stresa) — 30–45 min lakefront walk with views of the Borromean Islands. Then drive north via Domodossola and cross Simplon Pass (2,005 m) into Valais, Switzerland. Stalden sits on the Zermatt train line — walk to Stalden-Saas station tomorrow morning for Gornergrat.
