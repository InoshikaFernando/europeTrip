🎼 Salzburg + Hellbrunn · Sound of Music · ~10:30–14:00
Light first day — kids will be jet-lagged. Do the easy outdoor SoM hits, lunch, then push on. Skip Hohensalzburg funicular (too tiring on Day 1).
1
🕰️ Marienplatz + Glockenspiel (Munich · optional)
Marienplatz 8, 80331 München · ~40 min from MUC airport · free
Quick stretch in Munich's grand central square on the way out — see the New Town Hall & its famous Glockenspiel clock. Animated show at 11:00 & 12:00, but skip the wait and just enjoy the square. ⚠ Adds ~1½ hr — if you do it, the Salzburg times below shift ~1 hr later.
⏱ ~09:00–09:45 (optional)
2
Mirabell Gardens 🌹 ("Do-Re-Mi" finale)
Mirabellplatz 4, 5020 Salzburg · free · open daily
Pegasus fountain, gnome park, the famous staircase Maria runs up. Easy 30–45 min stroll. Perfect for stretched-out kids after the flight.
⏱ 10:30–11:15
3
Hellbrunner Allee
Hellbrunner Allee, 5020 Salzburg, Austria
Scenic tree-lined avenue south of Salzburg leading to Schloss Hellbrunn. Drive ~10 min from Mirabell · beautiful approach to the palace estate.
⏱ 11:15–11:30
4
🏰 Schloss Hellbrunn — trick fountains + 🎼 Sound of Music gazebo
Fürstenweg 37, 5020 Salzburg, Austria
🎼 The famous "I Am Sixteen Going on Seventeen" gazebo lives here · Trick Fountains (Wasserspiele) tour ~40 min with surprise water jets the kids will LOVE · €15/adult, kids cheaper · ⚠ Bring spare clothes — you WILL get wet · book at hellbrunn.at to skip queue.
⏱ 11:30–13:00 🎫 hellbrunn.at
5
Lunch · near Hellbrunn or back in Old Town
Café/restaurant near Schloss Hellbrunn · or quick drive to Old Town for Stiftskeller St. Peter (oldest restaurant in Europe, est. 803)
Hellbrunn has cafés on-site if you don't want to drive back. Otherwise short hop to Old Town for an iconic Salzburg lunch.
⏱ 13:00–14:00
6
Felsenreitschule (drive-by) — optional
Hofstallgasse 1, 5020 Salzburg
Baroque cliff theatre — the "Edelweiss" finale. 5-min walk from Stiftskeller. Skip if running late — the family escape Salzburg by 13:30 for Burg check-in.
⏱ 13:30 leave
