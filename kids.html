🤓 Did You Know?

🇩🇪 Germany

You'll cross the Autobahn — parts have NO speed limit! Don't get scared if cars zoom past.

🇦🇹 Austria · Hallstatt

Hallstatt is so pretty China built an exact copy of it in Guangdong!

🇨🇿 Czech Prague

The Astronomical Clock has been working since 1410 — that's 615+ years!

🇭🇺 Budapest

Budapest is actually TWO cities: Buda (hilly) + Pest (flat).

🇮🇹 Venice

Venice has 118 small islands and ZERO cars. Everyone uses boats!

🇻🇦 Vatican

The smallest country in the world — only 0.49 km². You can walk across it in 20 mins!

🇨🇭 Swiss Alps

Switzerland has so many cows, they outnumber people in some valleys!

🇫🇷 Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower grows 15 cm taller in summer because metal expands in heat!

🇳🇱 Amsterdam

There are more bicycles than people in Amsterdam (~880,000 bikes!).

🇩🇰 Copenhagen

Lego was invented in Denmark! The name means "play well" in Danish.

🇸🇪 Sweden

Swedes are the most cashless people in the world — they pay everything by card or phone!

🇨🇳 China · Great Wall

The Great Wall is over 21,000 km long — longer than the equator!
